## Direkte Top-Quotes

> „I write the way I would like to read."

> „If you can't remember the lesson, you might as well not have lived it and learned it."

> „The pain is the pitch. We sell at the point of greatest pain, not the point of greatest satisfaction."

> „If I can describe the pain so acutely, they're like — you can't know that pain this well and not be able to deliver."

> „Pain happens in moments. So I want to capture the moment."

> „Most people overemphasize promise. Pain is what motivates a lot of people to take action."

> „Validity: is this true? Utility: is this useful? Cut everything else."

> „If I can think of an example that doesn't fit in this, the model is wrong."

> „I have very little loyalty to my ideas. I am absolutely married to the truth, zero about how we get there."

> „Anything I would remove would materially detract from the substance — only then is it done. Until then: crunch more."

> „The internet doesn't really deal in nuance."

> „If you don't change behavior, you didn't learn."

> „The biggest filter I use for utility is whether it can be operationalized."

> „I talk almost zero about theory. I only talk about what you do."

> „How you launch a book determines how good you are at marketing. How well it's selling two years later determines how good the book is."

> „Building new products for your audience instead of building more audience for your product — that's the #1 mistake."

> „I want maximum comprehension rather than maximum word concision."

> „Short words. Short sentences. Big promises."

> „X is one of the best tools for learning to write — fast feedback, forced concision."

> „Hemingway and Stephen King's *On Writing* — the rest is repetition."
